## ✨ Try These Features

### Finance Module
1. Click **Finance** in the sidebar
2. Click **Add Transaction** button
3. Select Income or Expense
4. Fill in details and submit
5. See updated profit/loss automatically

### Create an Invoice
1. Click **Invoices** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Invoice/Quotation**
3. Fill in client details
4. Enter amounts
5. See auto-calculated totals

### Add an Employee
1. Click **HR Management**
2. Click **Add Employee**
3. Fill in personal and employment details
4. Submit to add to database

### Track a Lead
1. Click **Marketing**
2. Click **Add Lead** in the Leads section
3. Fill in lead information
4. Update lead status as it progresses

### Manage Inventory
1. Click **Inventory**
2. Click **Add Product** to add new items
3. Click **Stock Movement** to record in/out
4. See low stock alerts (highlighted in yellow)
